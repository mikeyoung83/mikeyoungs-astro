Conceptualized, designed and produced apps to be used on BlackBerry devices, under the Polar Mobile platform. As a graphic designer, I created the visual assets for applications to be used on smartphones (mainly BlackBerry and iPhone).

As part of the design team, my main responsibility was to take client leads (supported from the sales team), research their brand and website content, and to then produce the necessary graphical assets to be used in various BlackBerry handset applications.

This position required me to quickly become comfortable with systems I had never used before; such as the BlackBerry simulator and Git SVN file management.
