A lead generating website for business owners seeking loans, this was a fun project which began with a custom logo. Clean design and vivid people photography bring this site to life.  

![Homepage](./img/spark_1.png)
The key action for this site is to bring users to the information form. This is accomplished by funneling users down the page with useful information, till they are brought to a clear and concise call to action.

![Information](./img/spark_2.png)

In this case, data collection via a form.
